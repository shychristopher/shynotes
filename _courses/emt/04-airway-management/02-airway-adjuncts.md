---
title: OPA & NPA Adjuncts
description: Sizing, insertion, and contraindications for oropharyngeal and nasopharyngeal airways.
---

### Oropharyngeal Airway (OPA)
- **Indication:** Unresponsive patient **without** an intact gag reflex.
- **Sizing:** Corner of the mouth to the earlobe (or angle of the jaw).
- **Contraindication:** Intact gag reflex or responsive patient.

### Nasopharyngeal Airway (NPA)
- **Indication:** Semiconscious or unresponsive patient **with** an intact gag reflex.
- **Sizing:** Tip of the nose to the earlobe.
- **Contraindication:** Severe head/facial trauma (suspected basilar skull fracture / CSF leak).
