---
title: Manual Airway Maneuvers
description: Head-tilt/chin-lift vs. jaw-thrust indications and technique.
---

### Head-Tilt, Chin-Lift
- **Indication:** Unresponsive patient with **no suspected cervical spine injury**.
- **Technique:** Place one hand on the forehead to tilt the head back while lifting the bony prominence of the chin with fingertips (avoid compressing soft tissue under the chin).

### Jaw-Thrust Maneuver
- **Indication:** Unresponsive patient with **suspected spinal trauma** (MVC, fall, diving injury).
- **Technique:** Kneel at the top of the patient's head, place fingers behind the angles of the mandible, and displace the jaw anteriorly while keeping the cervical spine neutral.
