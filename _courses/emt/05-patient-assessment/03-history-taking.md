description: Structured medical history and symptom questioning mnemonics.
---

### SAMPLE History
- **S** — Signs & Symptoms
- **A** — Allergies
- **M** — Medications
- **P** — Past pertinent medical history
- **L** — Last oral intake
- **E** — Events leading up to the illness/injury

### OPQRST (Pain / Symptom Assessment)
- **O** — Onset
- **P** — Provocation / Palliation
- **Q** — Quality
- **R** — Region / Radiation
- **S** — Severity (0–10 scale)
- **T** — Time
