### Scene Size-Up Checklist
- **BSI / Standard Precautions:** Gloves, eye protection, mask/gown as indicated.
- **Scene Safety:** Traffic, hazards, violence, unstable structures, pets.
- **MOI / NOI:** Mechanism of injury (trauma) or nature of illness (medical).
- **Number of Patients:** Determine if MCI / triage protocols apply.
- **Additional Resources:** ALS intercept, fire, law enforcement, extrication.
- **C-Spine Stabilization:** Consider early based on MOI.
