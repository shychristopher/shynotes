### Respiratory Distress vs. Respiratory Failure
- **Distress:** Increased work of breathing, tripod positioning, accessory muscle use, tachypnea, patient still compensating.
- **Failure:** Altered mental status, bradypnea or shallow tidal volume, cyanosis, exhaustion — **assist ventilations immediately with BVM**.

### Common Breath Sounds
- **Wheezing:** Lower airway bronchoconstriction (asthma, COPD, anaphylaxis).
- **Crackles (Rales):** Fluid in alveoli (CHF / pulmonary edema).
- **Stridor:** High-pitched upper airway obstruction (croup, epiglottitis, foreign body).
