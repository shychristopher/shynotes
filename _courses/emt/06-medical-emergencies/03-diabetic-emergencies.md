---
title: Diabetic Emergencies
description: Hypoglycemia vs. hyperglycemia (DKA / HHNS) and oral glucose criteria.
---

### Hypoglycemia (Low Blood Glucose)
- **Onset:** Rapid (minutes to hours).
- **Signs:** Cool, clammy/diaphoretic skin, shakiness, confusion, combativeness, seizures, mimicking stroke or intoxication.
- **BLS Treatment:** Administer **oral glucose** if the patient is alert enough to maintain their own airway and swallow safely.

### Hyperglycemia (DKA / HHNS)
- **Onset:** Gradual (hours to days).
- **Signs:** Warm/dry skin, polydipsia/polyuria, nausea/vomiting, Kussmaul respirations, fruity acetone breath odor (DKA).
- **BLS Treatment:** Supportive airway/breathing care and prompt transport.
