### Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS)
- Classic presentation: substernal chest pressure/squeezing radiating to jaw, left arm, or back, often with diaphoresis, dyspnea, or nausea.
- **Atypical presentations** are common in older adults, women, and diabetic patients.

### Key BLS Interventions
- Place patient in a position of comfort and keep calm.
- Administer oxygen **only if hypoxic** (SpO₂ < 94%) or in respiratory distress.
- Assist with **aspirin** (162–324 mg chewable) per protocol if no contraindications.
- Assist with prescribed **nitroglycerin** if systolic BP meets protocol threshold and no recent PDE-5 inhibitor use.
