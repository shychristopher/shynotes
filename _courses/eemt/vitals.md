Quick-reference baseline ranges for exam prep and clinical checks.

| Age Group | Heart Rate (bpm) | Respiratory Rate (breaths/min) | Systolic BP (mm Hg) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Adult** | 60–100 | 12–20 | 90–120 |
| **Adolescent (12–18 yr)** | 60–100 | 12–20 | 90–110 |
| **School-Age (6–12 yr)** | 70–120 | 18–30 | 80–110 |
| **Toddler (1–3 yr)** | 90–150 | 24–40 | 70–100 |
| **Infant (1–12 mo)** | 100–160 | 30–60 | 70–95 |

### Additional Baselines
- **SpO₂:** 94%–99% on room air (target ≥ 94% unless COPD-specific guidance applies)
- **Blood Glucose:** 70–110 mg/dL (normal fasting range)
- **Capillary Refill:** < 2 seconds
