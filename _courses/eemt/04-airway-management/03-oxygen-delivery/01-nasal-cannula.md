---
title: Nasal Cannula
description: Low-flow oxygen delivery indications and flow rates.
---

- **Flow Rate:** 1–6 L/min
- **Delivered FiO₂:** ~24%–44%
- **Best For:** Mild hypoxia or patients who cannot tolerate a mask.
- **Notes:** Flow rates above 6 L/min dry out nasal mucosa without meaningfully increasing oxygen concentration.
