Airway patency and adequate ventilation always come first. Use the subtopics in this section to review manual maneuvers, adjunct sizing/contraindications, and oxygen delivery devices.

### Core Priorities
1. Confirm airway patency (look, listen, feel for obstructions or abnormal sounds).
2. Clear secretions or blood promptly with appropriate suctioning.
3. Maintain the airway using manual maneuvers and/or OPA/NPA adjuncts.
4. Deliver supplemental oxygen or assist ventilations via BVM when indicated.
