description: Identifying and treating immediate life threats (ABCs, AVPU, transport priority).
---

### AVPU Scale
- **A** — Alert (oriented to person, place, time, event — A&O×4)
- **V** — Verbal stimuli
- **P** — Painful stimuli
- **U** — Unresponsive

### Life Threats (X-ABCs)
- **X** — Exsanguinating hemorrhage (control immediately with direct pressure / tourniquet)
- **A** — Airway patency
- **B** — Breathing rate, depth, and effort
- **C** — Circulation (pulse, skin color/temp/condition, major bleeding)
