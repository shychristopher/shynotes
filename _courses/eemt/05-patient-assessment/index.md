Every call follows a consistent, repeatable structure so life threats are identified and treated immediately.

1. **Scene Size-Up** — BSI, scene safety, MOI/NOI, number of patients, additional resources, C-spine consideration.
2. **Primary Assessment** — General impression, AVPU, chief complaint, ABCs (or CAB for pulseless/apneic), transport priority.
3. **History Taking** — SAMPLE and OPQRST.
4. **Secondary Assessment** — Focused or rapid full-body exam + baseline vitals.
5. **Reassessment** — Every 5 min (unstable) or every 15 min (stable).
