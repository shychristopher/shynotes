Why I Built This

Notes shouldn't die in a forgotten folder.

I started {{ site.title }} because I kept putting real effort into distilling dense lectures, textbooks, and study guides—only for those notes to sit untouched on my laptop once the final was over.

Putting them out in the open forces me to write clearer explanations, gives me a fast reference I can pull up anywhere, and hopefully saves someone else a few late nights along the way.
